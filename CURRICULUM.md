# Curriculum: Beginner → Control Systems Engineer

Each item is about one lesson. Tick items off in `PROGRESS.md`, not here.

## Phase 1 — Electrical Basics (DC)
1. Charge, current, voltage, resistance, and Ohm's law
2. Power and energy (watts, joules, kWh)
3. Series circuits
4. Parallel circuits
5. Kirchhoff's current and voltage laws
6. Voltage dividers (the basis of many sensors)
7. Measuring with a multimeter: volts, amps, ohms, and doing it safely
8. Real components: resistors, switches, fuses, LEDs, batteries, power supplies

## Phase 2 — Components That Store Energy
9. Capacitors
10. Inductors
11. RC circuits and the time constant (**your first "system response"**)
12. RL circuits

## Phase 3 — AC Basics
13. What AC is: frequency, amplitude, RMS
14. Phase and sine waves
15. Impedance (resistance for AC)
16. Transformers, and why power grids use AC
17. Electrical safety: earthing, RCDs, circuit breakers

## Phase 4 — Maths Toolkit for Control
18. Algebra and rearranging equations (refresher)
19. Rates of change: an intuitive intro to derivatives
20. Accumulation: an intuitive intro to integrals
21. Differential equations: describing systems that change over time
22. Complex numbers
23. The Laplace transform, as a practical tool

## Phase 5 — Signals, Sensors and Actuators
24. Analog vs digital signals
25. Industrial signals: 0–10 V and 4–20 mA current loops
26. Sensors: temperature, pressure, level, flow, position
27. ADCs, DACs and sampling
28. Actuators: relays, contactors, motors, valves
29. Motor basics: DC motors, AC induction motors, VFDs

## Phase 6 — Control Fundamentals
30. Open-loop vs closed-loop control
31. Feedback and block diagrams
32. On/off (bang-bang) control and hysteresis
33. Proportional control and steady-state error
34. Integral and derivative action: PID
35. Transfer functions
36. First- and second-order system responses
37. Stability, overshoot and settling time
38. PID tuning methods (Ziegler–Nichols and others)

## Phase 7 — Industrial Practice
39. PLCs: what they are and how they scan
40. Ladder logic basics
41. HMIs and SCADA
42. Reading P&IDs and electrical schematics
43. Industrial comms: Modbus, Ethernet/IP, Profinet (overview)
44. Functional safety basics
