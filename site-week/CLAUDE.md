# Site Week — Claude instructions

This folder is a separate project from the control systems course in the repo root. The course instructions in `/CLAUDE.md` do not apply here.

## What this is
Chris's 7-day on-site plan: one hour a day across Move (25 min), Build (the 7pm Claude Lab session in `/claude-lab/`) and Eat (food rules, no extra time). The plan is in `PLAN.md`. Progress goes in `LOG.md`.

## When Chris reports a day
- Update his row in `LOG.md` from what he says. Ask only for what's missing.
- Reply in 3–5 short lines he can read on a phone: what he logged, and tomorrow's Move session, food rule from `PLAN.md`, and which Claude Lab night is next.
- A minimum day counts as done. Don't treat lost sleep as a win.
- Never guess numbers. If he didn't give one, leave the cell blank.
- Commit and push the log change.

## Style
Plain words, short sentences. No sports analogies. Times in Australia/Brisbane.
