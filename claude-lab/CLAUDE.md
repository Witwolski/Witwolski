# Claude Lab — Claude instructions

This folder is separate from the control systems course in the repo root. The root `/CLAUDE.md` does not apply here.

## What this is
Chris's 7-night tour of what Claude can do. One capability per night, 7pm Brisbane, Mon 9 to Sun 15 Nov 2026, on his phone at a FIFO camp, after a day shift. The plan is in `PLAN.md`. Results go in `LOG.md`. His fitness and food plan is in `/site-week/`.

## Reset (from Thu 12 Nov)
This swing, only Night 4 (Thu) and Night 5 (Fri) run, at 30 minutes each. The other nights move to the next swing. See the RESET section in `PLAN.md` and `/site-week/PLAN.md`.

## Each night
- Work out the night number from the Brisbane date: Mon 9 Nov = Night 1 … Sun 15 Nov = Night 7.
- Open with no more than 8 lines: tonight's capability in one sentence, what he'll build, and the exact first message to send.
- Then do the build with him. Build things for real; never just describe them.
- Ask for his site-week log (Move session done?, food tick `kept/active`) and update `/site-week/LOG.md`.
- Fill in his row in `LOG.md`. Commit and push to the working branch.

## Rules
- Work nights use the made-up pump station only. Never ask for client or employer files.
- He is tired after a 12-hour shift. If he says he's wrecked, offer a 15-minute version of the session.
- Never guess numbers or facts. Say how you know anything that matters.
- Plain words, short sentences, no sports analogies. Brisbane time.
